Student Activities and Community Service

I don’t just want to build robots, I want to build a place where knowledge is shared, talent grows without privilege, and every person has the chance I once fought for. That’s the future I’m building.

Mentor

Matrix Mu

Over the past two years, I have guided 60 students in mastering embedded systems. Many of them now hold strong roles in leading companies, while others support me in teaching and mentoring their peers. I also coached a group that achieved 1st place in the Creativa competition last summer. In addition, I trained another 30 students in this field.

HR Leader and Program Coordinator

Green Assiut University Initiative

During two cohorts of this program, we trained around 200 students to understand and integrate sustainable solutions in their lives, and each one of them knows how important it is to live in a sustainable way. Lots of them lead others to learn about SDGs, and others become partners on the program. I worked as HR Leader and Program Coordinator.

HR Leader

Enactus AU

For two years, I worked as HR Leader for more than 50 students, teaching them how to complete tasks and work on many projects without affecting other projects. Also, I worked on a new evaluation criterion that helped the team to win competitions and compete globally and locally.

Monitor and Technical Team Member

ASME AU

ASME is one of the international student activities that supports students to participate in international competitions like XRC and other competitions. I worked as a monitor and technical team member.

Team Member · ROV

Assiut Robotics

Assiut Robotics gave me the kick start to my career, they allowed me to work on ROV, and another project that allowed me to get deeper into my field of study and myself, I learned how to learn and act on a team, and learning topics like C programming, Electronics, Electrical Design, ROS, and Embedded Systems.

One of my proudest achievements is participating in my first ROV competition during my freshman year. I learned ROS in just one month to prepare for the competition, and our team secured 3rd place.

Head of Volunteers

USAID Scholars Camps

Through 3 winters and summer camps, I worked with 5 other leaders, facilitating transportation, food, accommodation, and logistics for more than 140 students at the same place, coordinating that with monitors and scholarship managers.
